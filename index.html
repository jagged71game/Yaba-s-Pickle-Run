Sound, controls & the pickle particulars
Your pantry mix

Use Sound on above to listen. Set music to zero for effects only. Settings apply for this visit. Sound starts only when you enable it.

Complete each level to unlock the next. Locked levels are marked in the selector; you can replay any unlocked level. Losing always retries the same level. Each begins with three fresh lives and has its own maze, rule card, personal best and completion stamp. Winning offers the next level; records are saved locally when storage is available. New run asks before erasing the saved campaign. Pause and choose Retry level to restart only this level and keep records. Press B or tap the brine button on levels 8–10 to release stored power. Purple paired rifts teleport Yaba, pink Wood Nymph flowers heal one missing life, and green Mycelic patches slow you down. Reverse Speed and Earthquake use the visible rule clock. Fog is lifted while brine is active. These are fan-game adaptations, not the main card-game rules.

Each pickle is worth 10 points. A brine barrel is worth 50 and gives seven seconds of power. While powered, tag different pursuers for 200, 400, then 800 points (800 for a fourth). Each pursuer scores once per brine burst; repeat catches send it home safely without points or combo growth. Four one-use barrels limit brine activations to four per level. Collect all pickles and reach the rift gate to win, with a bonus for time and remaining lives. Start each level with three lives. Earn one extra life at 1,500 and another at 3,500 points, once each per run (up to five held). The finish bonus does not award lives. At 25%, 50% and 75% rescued, optional Pickled Cherries (+150), Golden Gherkin (+300) and Wood Nymph’s Cordial (+100) appear on a cleared corridor. A maximum of three treats spawn per level, once each, worth 550 points total. Only one waits at a time; it does not expire. Losing a life does not replenish drops, barrels or healing flowers. Cordial restores a missing starting life, or gives a three-second safety shield if you already hold three or more. Bonus treats are never required to finish. Pursuers wake in stages so you can find your bearings. There is no time limit. Pause freezes all timers; changing tabs automatically pauses. Confirmed New run erases the saved campaign and starts Level 1. Your per-level bests and their accumulated total are shown in The Dill of Fame and saved in this browser when storage is available.

Arrow keys / WASD move. Space, P or Escape toggles pause. Enter starts from the title screen. Click or tap an open corridor to plot a visible route and stop at that destination. Routes do not avoid enemies: watch the chase and change your plan. Arrow keys, WASD, swipes and direction buttons take over immediately at the next tile. Tapping a rift routes to it; teleporting clears the route. Turning into a wall preserves your current direction until the next legal junction.
